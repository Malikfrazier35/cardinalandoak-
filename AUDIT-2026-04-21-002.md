## ⚠️ NEEDS USER ACTION — blockers outside the repo

These six items cannot be completed in the codebase. They require Malik to take real-world action. Ordered by sequence.

| # | Action | Why it blocks | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| U-1 | **File Cardinal & Oak, LLC with CT Secretary of State** | Site footer and all policy docs claim "Cardinal & Oak, LLC." Running ads as an LLC that doesn't exist is fraud, not just a technicality. | ~1 hour online. $120 filing fee. business.ct.gov. |
| U-2 | **Obtain EIN from IRS** | Bank account, tax filings, BOI all depend on this. | ~20 minutes online. Free. irs.gov/businesses. |
| U-3 | **File FinCEN BOI report** | Federally required within 30 days of LLC formation under the Corporate Transparency Act. | ~15 minutes. Free. boiefiling.fincen.gov. |
| U-4 | **Open dedicated business bank account** | Cannot commingle with personal/Financial Holding. Needed before first offer is extended (proof-of-funds letter) and first closing (funds source). | ~1–2 hours in branch or via online business banking. |
| U-5 | **Create Formspree account + swap endpoint into form** | Form currently POSTs to a placeholder. Leads go nowhere until this is done. See instructions below. | ~5 minutes total. |
| U-6 | **Bind minimum insurance: Commercial GL + Cyber** | Before first offer is extended. Cyber is most time-sensitive because the form collects PII. | 1 session with a business-insurance broker — most CT brokers can bind within 24 hours. |

## ⚙️ Formspree setup — the one code-adjacent step you need to do

1. Go to **https://formspree.io** → Sign up (free)
2. Verify your email `office@cardinalandoak.com` as the destination
3. Create a new form → name it "Cardinal & Oak — seller inquiries"
4. Formspree shows you an endpoint that looks like: `https://formspree.io/f/xyzabcde`
5. In your repo, open `index.html` and find this line:
   ```html
   <form action="https://formspree.io/f/REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_FORMSPREE_ID" method="POST">
   ```
6. Replace `REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_FORMSPREE_ID` with your actual Formspree form ID (just the `xyzabcde` part, or the whole URL — both work)
7. `git add index.html && git commit -m "wire form to Formspree" && git push`
8. Test: submit the form from your own phone. You should get an email at `office@cardinalandoak.com` within 30 seconds containing:
   - All form fields
   - `tcpa_consent: Yes, consented on submit`
   - Submitter's IP address (Formspree captures automatically — this is your TCPA defense)
   - Timestamp of submission

That's the entire TCPA-evidence capture. No Supabase, no edge functions, no schema. When/if volume grows past 50/month or you need a real CRM, we migrate.

## 📋 For the record — deferred items (Appendix A)

These items are legitimate parts of a mature compliance program. They are not necessary for a solo operator running a handful of CT transactions per year off inbound leads. Revisit if any of the following trigger:

- **Volume crosses ~25 transactions/year** → revisit AML policy, OFAC screening formalization, record-retention automation
- **Adding a second person to the firm** → revisit training docs, consistent-treatment logs, access controls
- **Moving into programmatic SEO or paid ads at scale** → revisit substantiation files, ad archive automation, claim-review cadence
- **Switching from "we buy" to wholesaling / assigning contracts** → entirely new compliance posture, re-audit required
- **Re-adding "Behind on payments" to marketing** → the full §4 foreclosure-rescue protocol becomes mandatory
- **Collecting more data types** (e.g. financial disclosures, negotiation notes) → revisit §3 privacy, DPAs, RLS
- **FinCEN RRE Rule gets reinstated on appeal** → ~30-day implementation window

The full 70-control framework is preserved in `COMPLIANCE.md` for when any of the above applies.
