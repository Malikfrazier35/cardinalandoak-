Policies

Equal Housing Opportunity.

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Cardinal & Oak does not discriminate against any person in the purchase of residential real property on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, marital status, lawful source of income, or any other characteristic protected by federal or Connecticut law.

Our commitment

We evaluate every property on its merits and every homeowner on the basis of the property and the circumstances, never on the basis of who they are. This commitment applies at every stage — initial inquiry, offer preparation, negotiation, and closing.

The laws that govern us

Cardinal & Oak complies with:

  • The federal Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. § 3601 et seq.)
  • The Connecticut Fair Housing Act (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 46a-64c)
  • The Connecticut Unfair Trade Practices Act (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 42-110a et seq.)
  • All other applicable federal, state, and local laws prohibiting housing discrimination

If you believe you've been discriminated against

If you believe Cardinal & Oak has treated you unfairly on the basis of any protected characteristic, we want to know. Please contact us directly at office@cardinalandoak.com so we can investigate.

You may also file a complaint with:

  • U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) — 1-800-669-9777 or hud.gov
  • Connecticut Commission on Human Rights and Opportunities (CHRO) — 1-800-477-5737 or portal.ct.gov/CHRO

Accessibility

If you require this information in an alternative format, or if you have difficulty communicating with us because of a disability, please contact us and we will accommodate you.
