## Audit finding #1 (Critical)

**Finding:** Homepage links to `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/fair-housing` return HTTP 404.

**Evidence:** Live fetch of `https://www.cardinalandoak.com/privacy` returned 404 at time of audit. Source files `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `fair-housing.html` exist in the repo, but Vercel's default static serving requires the `.html` extension unless `cleanUrls` is enabled.

**Affected controls:** §2.05 (EHO page live), §3.01 (Privacy published), §3.02 (Terms published), §3.03 (footer links), §3.04 (consent text link), §6.01 (non-discrim policy live).

**Why this is critical:** The intake form requires users to click through the Privacy Policy and Terms as part of TCPA consent. If those links 404, the consent is legally defective. Furthermore, an FTC deceptive-practices claim is strengthened by broken policy links on an active collection form.

**Remediation:** Add `vercel.json` (generated) to repo with `cleanUrls: true`. Push and verify all three pages resolve at their extensionless paths. This also adds HSTS and basic security headers.

**Status post-remediation:** Awaiting deploy.
